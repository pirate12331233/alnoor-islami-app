Chapter 1: Application Purpose & Overview

The Alnoor Islami mobile app serves as the official mobile portal of Al Noor International Trust. Designed to nourish the spiritual lives of members and the general public, it connects believers to daily Quranic recitation, Bukhari & Muslim Hadith, worldwide collective Darood Shareef recitations, high-definition live Mahafil broadcasts, and scholarly guidance in an ad-free, secure environment.

Purity Guarantee: The application contains zero third-party advertisements, zero monetization paywalls, and respects user privacy without selling or harvesting personal data.

Chapter 2: System Requirements & Installation

2.1 Supported Devices

  • Operating System: Android 7.0 (Nougat) up to Android 15+.
  • Memory (RAM): 2 GB minimum (3 GB+ recommended for background audio).
  • Storage Space: ~60 MB for initial installation, with ~150 MB buffer for offline Quran pages and audio caching.
  • Connectivity: Wi-Fi or Mobile Data (4G/5G). Essential functions (Quran, Prayer times, local Tasbih counter) work fully offline.

2.2 Step-by-Step APK Installation

1Download the official APK: Tap the verified download link from the official Alnoor website (alnoorislami.pk) or authorized WhatsApp channel.
2Grant Unknown Sources (One-Time): If prompted by Android, tap Settings and toggle Allow from this source.
3Complete Setup: Tap Install. When complete, tap Open.
Notification Permission (Android 13+): When the system dialog asks "Allow Alnoor Islami to send notifications?", choose ALLOW so you never miss prayer times, live broadcasts, or urgent trust announcements.

Chapter 3: Account Registration

Creating an account links your spiritual activity (such as your daily Darood tally) to your personal profile so your history is preserved even if you change phones.

3.1 Registration Options

  • Option A — Google Sign-In (Recommended): Tap the Continue with Google button. Select your Google account to log in immediately with zero password friction.
  • Option B — Email & Password: Tap Create Account, input your Name, valid Email address, and set a password with at least 6 characters.
  • Option C — Phone Number: Enter your mobile number prefixed with your country code (e.g. +92, +44, +1, +971).

Chapter 4: Login & Password Recovery

4.1 Logging In

Enter your registered Email/Phone and Password, then tap Sign In. The app maintains a persistent secure token so you remain signed in on your personal device.

4.2 Forgot Password Procedure

1On the login screen, tap the gold "Forgot Password?" link.
2Enter your registered email address and tap Send Password Reset Email.
3Open your email client, look for an email from Alnoor Trust, click the secure reset link, and define your new password.

Chapter 7: Worship Modules (Darood, Quran, Prayer)

7.1 Darood Counter Usage

The Darood Counter is designed to be effortless and mindful:

  • Haptic Tap: Tap the large green circle to count. The subtle vibration allows counting without staring at the screen.
  • Batch Increment: Tapping +33 or +100 lets you add recitations completed on physical beads.
  • Sending to Trust: Tap Submit Recitation when your session ends. Your tally is added to the global sum.

7.2 Quran-e-Pak Reader

  • Last Read Marker: Whenever you exit, your reading progress is automatically bookmarked down to the exact ayah.
  • Quick Search: Jump to any Surah or Parah in under two taps.

During active Mahafil, the Alnoor Islami Channel card displays the live link. Simply tap "Click to view Live Scheduled Mehfil" to enter the live stream in the YouTube app or browser.

Chapter 10: Troubleshooting FAQ & Support

Q1: Audio pauses when I turn off my phone screen?
Fix: Some phones (Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei) aggressively close background apps. Go to phone Settings > Apps > Alnoor Islami > Battery and set it to "Unrestricted" or "No restrictions".
Q2: Prayer times do not match my local mosque?
Fix: Ensure GPS is enabled. Go to Settings > Prayer Settings and select your regional Asr calculation school (Hanafi or Shafi'i).
Q3: APK download gets interrupted?
Fix: On the update screen, tap the collapsible "Having trouble? Alternative options" accordion and select "Download in Browser".
